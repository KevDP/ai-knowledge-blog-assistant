# About Kevin Delgado

Kevin Joan Delgado Pérez is a Robotics & Digital Systems engineer specialized in
AI/ML and Data Science. He designs and builds cloud-native AI systems on AWS,
combining production-grade ML pipelines with full-stack web work.

Trained at Tecnológico de Monterrey (ITESM) with a 3.7/4.0 GPA in Robotics
& Digital Systems Engineering and a 4.0/4.0 GPA in the AI minor, he has
~2 years of professional experience across enterprise ML and data projects.
He previously worked on the AI/ML team at AWS (as a Cloud Support Engineer)
and earlier as an AI & Data Analyst at Deloitte.

Kevin's focus is the intersection of **applied ML**, **cloud infrastructure**,
and **autonomous systems** — he is equally comfortable training an XGBoost
forecaster, packaging it behind a Lambda + API Gateway, and shipping the
frontend that consumes it.
